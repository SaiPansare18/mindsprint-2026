// THE MISSION BRIEF

SURVIVE THE CLOCK

ITM Skills University presents MindSprint 2026: a high-stakes 24-hour sprint pushing technologists, developers, and strategists to build functional MVPs before the impact clock hits zero.

PROTOCOL 01

Real-World Pressure

No theoretical presentations. Squads architect working software systems against a ticking 24-hour clock before the impact freeze.

PROTOCOL 02

Cross-Stream Synergy

Tech builders (B.Tech & MCA) unite with strategic commercial minds (BBA & MBA) to craft resilient, investable ventures.

PROTOCOL 03

Midnight Code Freeze

At 07:00 AM on Sunday, all commit pipelines lock. Demonstrations must be live, accessible, and ready for trial.

// THE DOOMSDAY PREMISE

WHAT IS DOOMSDAY?

Doomsday is the theme of MindSprint 2026: a simulated crisis where the clock is running out and the only way forward is to build. Every squad gets one problem, one deadline, and one shot at a working solution.

24
Hours on the clock
4
Programmes competing
12
Finalists on stage

Humanity

Crises hit people first. Build solutions that keep communities informed, connected, and supported when normal systems fail.

Tech

Technology is the toolkit. Engineers ship resilient, working software, and strategists make sure it can survive in the real world.

Planet

Disasters, scarcity, and disruption shape the world we build for. Ideas must hold up under real environmental and economic pressure.

Problem statements are revealed at 02:00 PM on Saturday, 21 November, in the Big Audi. Until then, the only thing you can prepare is your squad.
